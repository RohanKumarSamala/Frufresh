Our story

A legacy built over generations.
A business built for the world.

For over 50 years, our family has been deeply rooted in the fresh-produce business, building generations of expertise around fruit — its origins, seasons, quality, markets, and the relationships that bring it all together.

As our vision evolved, we expanded beyond India, building trusted partnerships with growers and suppliers across the world. Today, FRU FRESH sources premium fresh produce from 25+ countries and distributes across 8 states in India, connecting the right origins with the right quality and the right market at the right time.

This approach has earned us lasting relationships with leading organisations including Adani Group, Walmart Group, Reliance, and many more.

FRU FRESH is the next generation of a family legacy — bringing five decades of experience, trust and relationships together with a global outlook and a vision for what comes next.

Our roots are traditional, our sourcing is global, and our ambition is forward.

50+ years behind us. A world ahead of us.

The man behind the foundation

Vanne Venu Gopal

Founder, Chairman & Director — Fru Fresh

For more than 30 years, Vanne Venu Gopal has built his career in the fresh-produce industry around something that cannot be learned from a textbook — trust.

His journey began in the Indian fruit trade, long before FRU FRESH existed, through businesses including Vanne Agri Fresh, Sri Sai Fruit Agency and Vishnu Fruit Traders, where he built deep expertise through markets, customers and relationships.

Over the years, those relationships expanded far beyond India, creating a network that today spans 25+ countries, connecting FRU FRESH with growers, exporters, suppliers and partners across the global fresh-produce industry.

In Hyderabad’s fresh-produce trade, he is known as “The Big Boss” — a reputation built not simply through position, but through decades of experience, relationships and credibility.

His understanding of seasons, markets, quality and people has played a defining role in establishing FRU FRESH as a leading fresh-produce business in Hyderabad and Telangana, with a growing presence across 8 states in India.

For him, success has never been measured only in numbers, but in the suppliers who continue to trust him, the customers who continue to work with him, and the relationships that stand the test of time.

Today, he brings that experience and global network to FRU FRESH while working alongside the next generation to build its future.

Because the greatest thing he has built isn’t just a company — it’s a reputation.

The person behind Fru Fresh

Vanne Sai Pranav

I’ve spent most of my life around fruit. But I never wanted to just sell it.

I’m Vanne Sai Pranav, and FRU FRESH is my way of looking at an industry I’ve known all my life with a completely different perspective.

My family has been in the fresh-produce business for more than 50 years. Growing up around it, I got to see the business from a side most people never do — the early mornings, the markets, the conversations, the uncertainty of seasons and the incredible journey a fruit makes before it reaches you.

And the more I understood the business, the more curious I became.

What makes one origin better than another?
Why does the same fruit taste completely different depending on where it comes from?
And how do you bring that quality all the way to India and make it consistent?

Those questions became an obsession.

I started looking beyond the markets we already knew and towards the farms, growers and suppliers around the world.

That journey eventually became FRU FRESH.

Today, we source from 25+ countries and distribute across 8 states in India, building relationships across the entire supply chain and working with established organisations.

But for me, that’s only the beginning.

I don’t want FRU FRESH to be known simply as a company that imports fruit.

I want it to be known for knowing fruit.

Knowing where it comes from.
Knowing when it’s at its best.
Knowing what makes it special.
And knowing how to get it to India the right way.

I’m building FRU FRESH with something I inherited from my family — 50+ years of experience — but I’m building it with my own curiosity, ideas and ambition.

Because I believe the best way to respect a legacy isn’t to simply continue it.
It’s to see how far you can take it.

And that’s exactly what I’m here to do.
